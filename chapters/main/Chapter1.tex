	\caption{The Layers of Information Security.}
	\label{fig:1}
\end{figure}

The image illustrates a five-level cybersecurity protection pyramid, showing how different layers of security build upon each other.

At the base, the pyramid starts with Basic Security Protection, which emphasizes cyber hygiene and ensuring the safety of everyday internet users.

Above this is Critical Asset Protection, focusing on compliance with the NIS directive for \gls{dsp} and \gls{oes}.

The third layer, Digital Single Market Protection, covers threats such as cyber attacks, cyber crime, cyber espionage, and cyber sabotage.

The next tier, \gls{gsp}, broadens the scope to cyber norms, cyber diplomacy, cyber defence, and cyber warfare, highlighting their importance for international cybersecurity cooperation.
At the top of the pyramid, Democracy and Human Rights Protection addresses areas such as cyber ethics, cyber democracy, and cyber human rights, reinforcing the \gls{eu}’s core values in a digital society.



This layered structure demonstrates how essential security fundamentals support higher-level societal protections in the digital ecosystem \cite{R1,R2}.

\section{Related Work}\label{sec:related_work}

\subsection{Privacy Protection in Federated Learning}
Surveys of privacy-preserving FL for IoT intrusion detection group the techniques into differential privacy, homomorphic encryption, secure multi-party computation, trusted execution environments and blockchain~\cite{vyas2024}. The last three require heavy computation, special hardware or consensus overhead on constrained gateways, so this work uses DP and secure aggregation.

\textit{Differential privacy} limits how much a released output can change when one record is added or removed~\cite{dwork2014}. In the central model a trusted curator adds the noise; in the local model each client perturbs its own contribution and trusts nobody, at a much higher noise level. DP-SGD clips per-example gradients and adds Gaussian noise at every step~\cite{abadi2016dpsgd}; its cumulative loss is tracked with R\'enyi DP (RDP)~\cite{mironov2017rdp}.

\textit{Secure aggregation} lets the server learn only the sum of client vectors. The protocol of Bonawitz et~al.\ uses pairwise masks and secret sharing to tolerate dropouts~\cite{bonawitz2017}; SecAgg$^+$ replaces the complete communication graph with a sparse random graph and reaches polylogarithmic overhead~\cite{bell2020}. Secure aggregation adds no statistical noise, but it protects only the individual updates, not the aggregate.

\textit{Distributed DP} combines both: each client adds a share of the noise and secure aggregation reveals only the sum, which then carries the full calibrated noise. Because secure aggregation works on integers modulo $2^b$, discrete mechanisms are needed. Kairouz et~al.\ proposed the distributed discrete Gaussian~\cite{kairouz2021}. Agarwal et~al.\ showed that the Skellam distribution is closed under summation and gave a tight RDP bound for it~\cite{agarwal2021}. Both works train models with many rounds; a release made of a few sums, such as count tables, is a simpler fit.

\subsection{Synthetic Data for Intrusion Detection}
Oversampling such as SMOTE interpolates between minority neighbors~\cite{chawla2002}, which requires the minority records to be pooled and is therefore unavailable when a gateway holds few or no attack records. A systematic review of generative AI for IoT intrusion detection found that most studies use generative adversarial networks (GANs) or variational autoencoders for data augmentation and class rebalancing, and noted mode collapse and training instability as recurring problems~\cite{deng2025}.

Federated generative IDSs follow this line. Hamouda et~al.\ trained a federated conditional GAN on Edge-IIoTset and applied DP-SGD to the downstream classifier rather than to the generator; they identified cryptographic protection of the models as an open challenge~\cite{hamouda2024}. Yao et~al.\ protected the downlink broadcast of synthetic samples with dynamic $(t, n)$-threshold secret sharing, while the uplink gradients relied on Gaussian DP~\cite{yao2024}. Shettar et~al.\ reported a detection F1-score of 96.8\% with a central aggregator under DP at $\varepsilon = 4.8$~\cite{shettar2026}. None of these works applies secure aggregation to the generator updates or measures how DP and secure aggregation each affect rare-class detection.

\subsection{Marginal-Based Private Synthesis}
Marginal-based synthesizers avoid backpropagation altogether. PrivBayes fits a Bayesian network to noisy low-order marginals~\cite{zhang2017privbayes}; MST measures noisy two-way marginals along a maximum spanning tree and post-processes them with a graphical model~\cite{mckenna2021mst}; AIM selects marginals adaptively and iteratively~\cite{mckenna2024aim}. Two works move this family to distributed data. FLAIM adapts AIM to federated clients under zero-concentrated DP and compares it with a secure multi-party variant~\cite{maddock2024}. CaPS performs marginal selection and measurement inside secure multi-party computation (``DP-in-MPC'')~\cite{pentyala2024}. Both were evaluated mainly on general-purpose tabular benchmarks from demographic, financial and biomedical domains; FLAIM also used the legacy DARPA intrusion detection data, but measured workload error and negative log-likelihood rather than downstream rare-class detection. The family has known limits: only low-order dependencies are modeled, continuous attributes must be discretized, and sparse cells have a low signal-to-noise ratio under DP.

\subsection{Research Gap}
Table~\ref{tab:lit_review} summarizes the closest works. Based on a non-systematic search of the literature, and to the best of our knowledge, no prior study (i) evaluates a federated marginal generator on IoT-MQTT traffic for downstream rare-class detection, (ii) compares it with a federated deep generator under identical partitions, budgets and secure aggregation, or (iii) combines distributed discrete DP with a real secure aggregation implementation and reports the guarantee as a function of the number of honest clients.
